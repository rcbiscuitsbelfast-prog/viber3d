To use custom 3D fonts in your dashboard, place .ttf font files here.

Recommended sources:
- MedievalSharp: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/MedievalSharp
- Uncial Antiqua: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Uncial+Antiqua

Download the .ttf files from Google Fonts and place them in this folder as:
- MedievalSharp-Regular.ttf
- UncialAntiqua-Regular.ttf

Once added, your sign text will render in 3D with these fonts.
