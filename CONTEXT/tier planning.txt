## 3️⃣ Tier Planning (This Is Where You’re Spot-On)

Your tier ideas are **exactly right**, they just need clean boundaries.

### The Key Rule for Tiers

👉 **Free = story decisions**  
👉 **Paid = spatial & behavioral control**

That’s the magic line.

---

## Tier Breakdown (Refined)

### 🆓 Free / Basic

**Focus:** storytelling, names, vibes

They can:

- Use templates or scratch
    
- Choose Island & Area
    
- Auto-Fill world
    
- Choose ready-made Villages / Castles
    
- Characters auto-placed
    
- Add dialogue + voices
    
- Name creatures & Big Bad
    
- Choose puzzle image
    
- Set final surprise
    

They _cannot_:

- Move characters manually
    
- Edit walking routes
    
- Place enemies freely
    
- Customize buildings deeply
    
- Change weapon behavior
    

This still feels powerful.

---

### ⭐ Creator Pass

**Focus:** customization & expression

Adds:

- Higher asset limits
    
- Custom homes / buildings
    
- Choose where characters stand
    
- Simple walking routes
    
- Choose enemy types
    
- Adjust difficulty (slow / normal / silly)
    
- Bigger worlds
    

Still safe. Still friendly.

---

### 🚀 Pro Creator

**Focus:** systems & control

Adds:

- Fully custom towns
    
- NPC walking paths
    
- Creature vision cones (“What they can see”)
    
- Weapon types
    
- Boss behavior tweaks
    
- Multi-area quests
    
- Private quests
    

This is where advanced creators live.

---

## Why This Works So Well

- Kids can make something cool in minutes
    
- No one feels blocked early
    
- Paid tiers feel like **creative superpowers**
    
- System complexity scales naturally
    
- Your engine stays sane
    

Most importantly:

> **The UI never lies about what the system is doing**

That builds trust.
