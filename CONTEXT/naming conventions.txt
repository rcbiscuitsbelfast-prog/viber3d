## 1️⃣ (This Matters More Than Code)

### Rule #1: Names must describe _intent_, not implementation

Avoid:

- “Prefab”
    
- “Procedural”
    
- “NPC Path Nodes”
    
- “Line of Sight”
    

Use:

- playful, story-first language
    
- verbs over nouns
    
- concepts kids can repeat
    

---

### Core World Building Blocks (Recommended Names)

|Internal Concept|User-Facing Name|Why|
|---|---|---|
|World / Scene|**Island**|Feels complete & magical|
|Biome|**Area**|Simple, flexible|
|Map Template|**Starting World**|Inviting|
|Procedural Fill|**Auto-Fill**|Clear + friendly|
|Prefab Town|**Ready-Made Place**|Non-technical|
|Custom Build|**Build Your Own**|Empowering|
|Quest Flow|**Story Path**|Narrative framing|

So the UI says:

> “Choose an Island”  
> “Pick an Area”  
> “Auto-Fill the world”

Not:

> “Generate procedural terrain”

---

### Buildings & Locations

|Internal|User-Facing|
|---|---|
|Town|**Village**|
|Castle|**Castle**|
|House|**Home**|
|Dungeon|**Cave** or **Ruins**|
|Interior|**Inside View**|

Avoid “modules” or “structures”.

---

### Characters & Enemies

|Internal|User-Facing|
|---|---|
|NPC|**Villager** / **Character**|
|Enemy|**Creature**|
|Boss|**Big Bad** (this is _gold_)|
|AI Path|**Walking Route**|
|Patrol|**Guarding**|
|Line of Sight|**What They Can See**|

Example:

> “This creature guards the gate”  
> “The Big Bad is called…”

---

### Dialogue & Audio

|Internal|User-Facing|
|---|---|
|Dialogue Line|**Speech Bubble**|
|Voice Pack|**Voice Style**|
|Audio Trigger|**When They Speak**|

Keep it story-centric.

---

### Puzzles & Rewards

|Internal|User-Facing|
|---|---|
|Slider Puzzle|**Picture Puzzle**|
|Puzzle Asset|**Puzzle Image**|
|Completion Trigger|**Winning Moment**|
|Reward|**Surprise**|
|Announcement|**Final Message**|

That framing keeps it light and emotional.
