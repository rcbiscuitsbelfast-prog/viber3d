
## 2️⃣ Creation Flow (This Is Excellent — Just Needs Framing)

Your idea of **Template vs Scratch** is exactly right.

### Creation Entry Screen (Mentally)

> **How do you want to start?**

- 🏰 Use a Ready-Made Quest
    
- 🧱 Build One From Scratch
    

No jargon. No pressure.

---

### If They Choose Template

Flow:

1. Pick **Island**
    
2. Pick **Quest Type** (Find / Help / Defeat / Solve)
    
3. Auto-Fill world
    
4. Customize names, voices, reward
    
5. Share
    

This is your **5-minute win path**.

---

### If They Choose Build From Scratch

Flow:

1. Choose Island + Area
    
2. Auto-Fill (optional)
    
3. Add Places (Village, Castle, Cave)
    
4. Add Characters
    
5. Add Creatures / Puzzles
    
6. Set Winning Moment
    

Same system — more freedom.
