## 🧠 Why This Screen Works

### 1️⃣ No tables, no math

People don’t want to compare 12 variables.  
They want to recognize themselves in a tier.

---

### 2️⃣ Emotional framing > feature lists

Each tier answers:

> “Who is this for?”

Not:

> “What do I get per dollar?”

---

### 3️⃣ Ownership stands alone

“Own a Quest Forever” being separate is _brilliant_ because:

- It doesn’t feel like a downgrade
    
- It avoids subscription fatigue
    
- It feels like buying a thing, not renting access
    

---

### 4️⃣ Free doesn’t feel bad

You’re not shaming free users.  
You’re inviting them to upgrade _when they care_.

That builds trust.

---

## ⚠️ What NOT to Put on This Screen

Avoid:

- Play counts
    
- Day limits
    
- Storage MB
    
- Technical terms
    
- “No ads” (unless true)
    

Those belong in tooltips or FAQs — not the main decision moment.
